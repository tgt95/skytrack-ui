> In-app tabbed workspace supporting intensive multitasking across multiple documents without state loss.

## Overview

Enables opening and switching between multiple documents and records within the application, avoiding browser tab explosion and preserving draft states.

## Usage

- Core banking systems, logistics dispatch desks, and high-frequency CRM tele-sales.
- Preserves unsaved form state and scroll positions per tab.

**Live demo variant:** `Default`
